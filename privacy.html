Privacy in Mom, It's a Startup

The game collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your progress and settings stay in your browser’s storage on this device. You can erase your progress with “Reset game.”

The game uses the network only to load or update its files. Your save never leaves your device. GitHub Pages hosts those files and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security when it serves them. You can read more in GitHub’s hosting documentation.

For a privacy question, open an issue on the game’s support page. Issues are public, so please leave out personal details.
